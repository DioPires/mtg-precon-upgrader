Star Trek prerelease cheat sheet (DEC-1218)

Sealed grades, 0 to 10, from oracle text. I checked them against MTG Arena Zone's Draft review on 2 November (49 cards re-read, 20 changed) and against Draftsim's ratings on 3 November (18 re-read, 11 changed). Our uncommons sit 0.58 grade above our commons, under the 1.0 target; the two reviews' gaps are 0.51 and 0.23: between two cards half a grade apart, prefer the uncommon. ★ bomb · ✕ removal · ⚡ instant-speed · ◆ fixing · ✚ card advantage · ⚪🔵⚫🔴🟢 colour · 🟡 gold · ◇ colourless.

1. Scoring your pool by hand (10 minutes)

  1. Sort by colour. A hybrid card counts for either colour; put gold cards aside.
  2. Count per colour: cards graded 6+, cards graded 4+, and bombs (★). Write them on the deck box.
  3. Pick the two colours with the most 6+ cards. A ★ counts as two. Add the pair's gold cards.
  4. Check: 22-23 cards graded 4+ and 15+ creatures. Count the pair's two colours, its gold cards and your colourless cards. Spacecraft are not creatures. If short, look at the next colour or a splash.
  5. Splash up to 3 cards graded 6+ with 3+ sources of their colour, at most 1 of them a basic. Sources: duals and shock lands that make it, Class-F Shuttlecraft, Xenobotanist. With 4+ non-basic sources, the third colour may also fill step 4, with cards of 3+ mana.
  6. Build 17 lands and 23 spells, with 7-8 two-drops. Play the ✕ you have, but do not pick colours for it.
  7. Tie between pairs? Pick more ★, then more ✚, then more ✕, then more two-drops.

2. Format rules that decide games

  • Station: tap another creature, at sorcery speed, to put charge counters equal to its power on a Spacecraft. At the number on its stripe it becomes an artifact creature. 17 Spacecraft, 3 of them common.
  • A creature tapped to station stays tapped through your opponent's turn and cannot block. It may have entered this turn. Attack with vigilance creatures first, then station in your second main phase.
  • A Spacecraft below its number is not a creature. Creature removal cannot hit it and it cannot block. 70,000 Light-Years from Home and Guidance Failure hit Spacecraft too.
  • Face a dilemma = choose a mode. Each modal spell or ability ("choose one —") counts once as you choose, even if it is countered later; with no legal mode it does not count. 39 cards have modes (14 commons); 9 cards pay off.
  • Beam me up: cast the sorcery again from your graveyard and return one of your creatures to your hand as part of the cost. The spell is then exiled. 10 sorceries have it (5 commons); Scotty, Miracle Worker gives it to your graveyard. Return a creature with an enters ability to use it again.
  • Strange new worlds counts the different land names you control. Each basic type counts once, each Planet dual once, all New Planet tokens once. A two-colour deck with one dual counts 3. 13 cards.
  • Federation counts the creature types among your non-Borg creatures. Officer tokens add Officer. 6 cards, none common; Saavik's damage uses it.
  • A stun counter keeps a creature tapped through its next untap. 6 cards; Constable Odo puts two.
  • Planet duals enter tapped and gain you 1 life. A pool holds about 3 (one in two packs, land slot only).
  • 3 damage kills 75% of creatures; 4 damage kills 88%. In combat, respect open W, 1B and 2R: Set Phasers to . . ., Eject the Warp Core, Plasma Cascade.

5. The ten archetypes

Unranked: your pool decides. Archetype names come from Draftsim's archetype guide (22 October). Look for: our picks from card text, including Draftsim's signposts graded under 5.

Pair plans (signposts = this pair's two-colour cards graded 5+; grades in brackets):

⚪🔵 WU Spacecraft

— Station Spacecraft with creatures you do not need on defence; Hemmer loots each time it taps and gives your Spacecraft hexproof on your turn.

Signposts:

Constable Odo [6]; Hemmer, Fixing What Is Broken [5.5]

Look for:

U.S.S. Excelsior, Cutting Edge [6.5], U.S.S. Cerritos, Following Up [6], Enterprise NX-01 [5], Melkotian Adjudicator [5], Shuttle Ace [5], Suurok-Class Science Vessel [4.5], Class-F Shuttlecraft [4.5].

🔵⚫ UB Dilemmas

— Play cards with modes: each mode you choose triggers Seven of Nine, Melkotian Adjudicator and the other dilemma cards.

Signposts:

Mirror Universe Intrusion [6]; Seven of Nine [6]; Lore, Positronic Provocateur [5]

Look for:

Resistance Is Futile [7], Impossible Choice [6], Gabriel Lorca, Mirror Menace [5.5], Melkotian Adjudicator [5], Consider the Prime Directive [5], Rogue Artificial Intelligence [5], Tal Shiar Operative [4], Obsidian Order Operative [4].

⚫🔴 BR Klingon Sacrifice

— Attack every turn with Klingon tokens and cheap creatures, and sacrifice the ones that would die anyway to draw or surveil.

Signposts:

Gowron, Eager for Battle [6]; L'Rell, Fierce Visionary [6]; The Duras Sisters [5.5]

Look for:

Eject the Warp Core [6], Cha'DIch Investigator [5.5], Automated Warfare System [5], Sarcophagus, Ship of the Dead [4.5], Battle-Scarred Survivalist [4], Boarding Action [4], Test of Identity [4].

🔴🟢 RG Landfall

— Play a land every turn: each one makes a Worm, a counter or damage, and extra land drops (U.S.S. Voyager, Erica Ortegas) trigger it again.

Signposts:

Cleveland Booker, Empath [6]; Dal R'El, Augment Orphan [5.5]

Look for:

Syndicate Liquidators [6], Captain Michael Burnham [5.5], U.S.S. Voyager, Lone Wanderer [5.5], Exploratory Mission [5], Xindi Surveyors [4.5], Saurian Explorer [4.5], Erica Ortegas, Plucky Pilot [4.5].

⚪🟢 WG Counters

— Put +1/+1 counters on several creatures and attack: Phlox draws for each one that connects, and The Doctor adds a counter to each.

Signposts:

Phlox, Unorthodox Doctor [6.5]; The Doctor, EMH [5.5]; The Inner Light [5.5]

Look for:

Cold-Blooded Crew [5.5], Culber and Stamets, Star-Crossed [5.5], Test of Conviction [5], Dr. McCoy, Surly Surgeon [5], Dominion Supervisor [4.5], Federation Field Medic [4.5], Support Mission [4.5], Pure Energy [4].

⚪⚫ WB Artifact Creatures

— Make Borg artifact creatures and tokens, then pump them together with Locutus and Assault Drone.

Signposts:

Borg Cube [7]; Locutus, the One Who Speaks [6]; Zero, Incorporeal Sensate [5]

Look for:

Resistance Is Futile [7], Hive Mind Coprocessor [5.5], Assembly Specialist [5], Conquest Mission [4.5], Assault Drone [4.5], Tenacious Drone [4], Collective Drone [4].

— Cast removal, Spacecraft and other noncreature spells to make Officers, counters and Gray and Adira copies; Spock finds the next spell.

Signposts:

Spock, Always Your Friend [5]

Look for:

Transporter Malfunction [7], Plasma Cascade [6.5], Consider the Prime Directive [5], Academy Instructor [4.5], Cetacean Ops [4.5], Anthropological Mission [4.5], Tal Shiar Analyst [4], Gray and Adira, Forever Joined [4.5].

⚫🟢 BG Leaves the Graveyard

— Surveil and mill to stock your graveyard, then cast, exile or return cards from it; Vash draws a card the first time each turn one leaves.

Signposts:

Nog, Ambitious Cadet [6]; Vash, Freelance Archaeologist [6]; Daniels, Temporal Agent [5.5]

Look for:

🔵🟢 UG Strange New Worlds

— Play many differently named lands to grow The Traveler and cut the cost of your strange-new-worlds cards; green holds 4 of the 6 coloured common and uncommon fixers, so add a third colour here.

Signposts:
