# P2.4 — Fleet precompute + EDHREC crowd prior (21 decks)

**Requirements:** RK-2 (crowd-prior term), DR-4

## Objective
Precompute per-fleet-deck Tier-C candidates (like `spellbook-combos.json`),
baking the EDHREC crowd prior into the ranking. Extend the EDHREC cache to all 21
fleet decks (DEC-410 low-volume posture).

## Deliverables
- `priv_data/edhrec/*.json` — 21 fleet decks (13 new fetches via
  `scripts/fetch_edhrec_rates.py`; gitignored, not shipped).
- `scripts/build_synergy_candidates.py` — emits
  `data/commanders/{id}/synergy-candidates.json` per fleet deck.

## Acceptance
- EDHREC prior applied at **build time** (Python). Raw rates stay in gitignored
  `priv_data/` — only the resulting coarse band / small prior contribution is
  committed. No EDHREC rates ship to the browser (decision 4 hard gate:
  public-deployment terms exposure is a board re-review, out of scope here).
- Paste mode (client engine) omits the prior entirely.
- Committed per-deck JSON is plain, stable-ordered, small.
