# P2.6 — KB-gap caveat (uncovered cards)

**Requirements:** DC-6

## Objective
Cards that resolve by name but lack a KB / feature-index entry are marked
**uncovered** in the report — distinct from IN-6 unresolvable lines (a name that
does not resolve to any card at all).

## Acceptance
- Engine output carries an `uncovered[]` list: resolvable card names with no
  feature-index entry.
- The report distinguishes uncovered (has a card, no KB semantics yet — full
  coverage is a Phase-4 entry gate) from unresolved (IN-6, no card).
- Never guessed: an uncovered card contributes no edges and is disclosed, not
  silently dropped.
