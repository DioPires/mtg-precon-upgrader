# P2.2 — Candidate generation: pairs, triples, 4-cycles

**Requirements:** DC-2, DC-5, NF-3

## Objective
Enumerate synergy candidates over the deck's provides/wants graph: pairs at
minimum; triples where a two-card match is completed by a third capability;
4-cycles as the Tier-B cap contingency (plan §Tier-B cycle-piece cap).

## Deliverables
- `combo_synergy_lib.enumerate_candidates(graph, depth)` — pairs, triples,
  4-cycles. Cycle enumeration over the sparse typed graph, NOT card-subset
  brute force (C(100,4) is prohibited by NM-4/DC-5).

## Acceptance
- Pairs: matched provides/wants edges (DC-1).
- Triples: an unmatched want closed by a third card's provide, or a 3-cycle in
  the typed graph (DC-2, DC-5 candidate promotion).
- 4-cycles: cycles of length 4 in the provides/wants graph (funded by plan; feeds
  Phase-3 verification cap contingency).
- Enumeration stays within the interactive-latency budget for a 100-card list
  (NF-3 enumeration slice): linear-ish in edges, capped fan-out.
