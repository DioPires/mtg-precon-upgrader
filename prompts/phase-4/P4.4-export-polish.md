# P4.4 — Export/share polish (RT-4)

**Requirements:** RT-4 (exportable/shareable preserving tier labels + attribution; at minimum plain text
AND a shareable rendering; labels must survive every path — Section 10).

Extends `web/src/lib/spellbookReport.ts`:
- `buildPlainTextExport(report, completions?)` now appends the computed near-miss completion sections
  (Tier B + Tier C), each keeping its model-conditional / speculative framing and tier label.
- New `buildMarkdownExport(report, completions?)` — the shareable rendering. Renders cleanly on
  Reddit/Discord/GitHub with headings, preserving every tier badge, the epistemic-tier preamble, corpus
  version, prices, and full attribution (Spellbook + Scryfall + price sources + WotC fan-content line).

Tier labels are written inline in both paths (EI-4): a shared report stripped of epistemic labels
violates Section 10.
