# P1.5 — Sampling QA (QE-5 / KB-7)

**Requirements:** QE-5, KB-7

## Objective
Measured QA over the KB with a stricter bar for KB-7 high-impact cards (those in
a Spellbook combo variant — they feed Tier-B verdicts).

## Deliverables
- `scripts/kb_sampling_qa.py` — automatic fidelity report + stratified human-review worksheet

## Acceptance
- Automatic checks: evidence occurs in oracle text; magnitudes positive;
  provenance in vocabulary; activated costs occur in oracle text.
- Pass rates reported overall and for the KB-7 subset.
- Worksheet oversamples KB-7, seeded for reproducibility.
