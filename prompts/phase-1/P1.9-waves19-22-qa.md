## Method
- **Selection:** deterministic, reproducible order — oracle_id ascending over the still-`needs_llm`
  set (the local mirror carries no `edhrec_rank`; oracle_id is the stable, auditable signal).
  Emitted **all 1,162** remaining `needs_llm` prompts in one pass (`classify_combo_kb.py
  --emit-batch`, no `--limit`) and split by order: Wave 19 = cards 1–300 (`Forced Retreat` ..
  `Champion of the Flame`), Wave 20 = 301–600 (`Organic Extinction` .. `Abzan Advantage`),
  Wave 21 = 601–900 (`Stronghold Confessor` .. `Quandrix Charm`), Wave 22 = 901–1,162
  (`Ordruun Commando` .. `Without Weakness`).
- **Classifier:** offline build-time batch (KB-5 / NF-1 — never on the query path). Each card's
  prompt is emitted by `classify_combo_kb.py` (IN-7-hardened: card oracle text sanitised of
  fence-forgery + role-injection and fenced as untrusted data; the system instruction forbids
  treating fenced text as directives), classified against the **closed v1.0.1 ontology (30
  features)** at 100 cards per unit (12 units × 100/62 = 1,162), then `--apply` per wave.
- **Provenance:** every output stamped `llm` → Tier-C capped, **never** Tier-B-eligible until
  human review (`TIER_B_PROVENANCES = {deterministic, human-reviewed}`; `'llm'` ∉ that set,
  verified directly).
- **High empty rate is correct by design.** 926 / 1,162 cards classify empty because a long-tail,
  non-combo card's mechanics usually fall outside the v1.0.1 combo-economy ontology. Such cards are
  logged as QE-3 ontology gaps rather than force-fit to an ill-fitting feature — empty ≠ missed;
  empty = "no ontology-expressible combo signal."

## Reviewer notes (judgment calls flagged by the offline classifiers — all Tier-C, for human review)
These are the standing looser-fit calls the long tail forces; none affect vocab/economy/evidence
integrity (all pass machine validation), and all are Tier-C capped:
- **Cycling / type-cycling reminder text** ("Draw a card.") tagged `draw` on several cards — a

  broader than the ontology's `{X}{X}:` example, but the literal "repeatable mana sink" reading.

  "team keyword grant" but narrower than a full-team anthem.
- **Non-`+1/+1` counters** (-1/-1, stun, level, wither reminder text) tagged `counter_add` under
  the ontology's "+1/+1, charge, etc." — the "etc." read inclusively.
- **Sacrifice-as-spell-cost** (Casualty / additional-cost sac) tagged `sac_outlet` under the
  "activated ability / cost" wording — a one-shot cost, not a repeatable permanent outlet.
- **Prowess / magecraft keyword** tagged `cast_trigger` — a keyword rather than written-out
  "whenever you cast."
- **"controller loses life"** riders left **untagged** for `drain` unless the text says "opponent"
  / "each opponent" — conservative per the ontology's opponent-scoped definition.
