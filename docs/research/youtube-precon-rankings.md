# Content Creator Favorite Commander Precons - YouTube Research

> Research compiled from YouTube content creators including Tolarian Community College, Nitpicking Nerds, and The Command Zone.
>
> Date: January 2025

---

## Top-Tier Precons According to Content Creators

### #1: Eldrazi Unbound (Modern Horizons 3)
**Source:** Tolarian Community College (641K views)

**Why creators love it:**
- First-ever entirely colorless precon - unprecedented design
- Ulalek, Fused Atrocity gives spells 7+ mana **double cascade**
- Kozilek, the Great Distortion is "an absolute house" - refills hand, counters spells
- Exceptional mana base with Urza Tron lands, utility lands
- Massive ramp package - competitive even with mono-green decks
- "Rise of the Eldrazi" is a 12-mana bomb: removal + card draw + extra turn
- **The Professor's verdict:** "No doubt - the strongest Commander precon ever made"

---

### #2: Necron Dynasties (Warhammer 40k)
**Source:** Tolarian Community College

**Why creators love it:**
- Mono-black artifact creatures with graveyard recursion
- **Never stumbles on mana** - sleek mono-color base
- Living Death and "Their Name is Death" are effectively one-sided board wipes
- Hard to interact with due to constant recursion
- Late game dominance with Gilded Lotus, Scepter of Eternal Glory
- **Key insight:** Swap out the face commander for any of the 4 alternates for even better performance

---

### #3: Party Time (Battle for Baldur's Gate)
**Source:** Tolarian Community College

**Why creators love it:**
- Nalia de'Arnise - 3-mana engine with **no once-per-turn clause**
- 44 creatures all contribute to party (Warrior, Wizard, Cleric, Rogue)
- Premium reprints: Mother of Runes, Skull Clamp, Deep Gnome Terramancer, Black Market Connections
- $127 singles value at $68 sealed price (2x value)
- "Even historical greats would struggle to keep up with it"

---

### #4: Timey-Wimey (Doctor Who)
**Source:** Tolarian Community College

**Why creators love it:**
- Two-commander format gives an extra card in hand
- Suspend + Time Travel lets you cheat massive spells onto the stack early
- All commanders synergize with the deck theme
- Includes **Farewell** reprint - "notorious for how freaking strong it is"
- "Everybody Lives" emergency button (comparable to Teferi's Protection)
- Exceptionally tight 99 with very few non-synergistic cards

---

### #5: Urza's Iron Alliance (Brothers War)
**Source:** Tolarian Community College

**Why creators love it:**
- Urza has affinity for artifacts - hard to keep off battlefield
- 17 two-mana spells for consistent early plays
- 10 pieces of early mana acceleration
- Includes Swords to Plowshares and 3 board wipes
- "I would feel very confident bringing this deck even if it was the only precon at the table"

## Best Current-Value Precons (2024-2025)

### Hearthole the World Seed (Edge of Eternities) - $50
**Source:** Nitpicking Nerds

**Why it stands out:**
- **Completely unique** land-sacrifice theme - "unheard of 10 years ago"
- Aftermath Analyst, Gitrog Monster, Horizon Explorer reprints
- Cohesive deck that "asks nothing of you"
- Add free sac outlets to take deck to "another level"
- **Verdict:** "One of only 2 worst cards in the whole deck"

---

### Zenia, Valley's Voice / Family Matters (Bloomburrow) - $37
**Source:** Nitpicking Nerds

**Why it stands out:**
- **Cheapest recommended precon** at just $37
- $101 total singles value (almost 3x ratio!)
- Offspring mechanic doubles ETBs
- Agot Instigator, Polliwog Prodigy ($10), Helm of the Host ($13)
- Junk Winder locks down boards
- Zenia can easily be a one-hit kill (10+ power flyer)

---

### Shiko and Narset Unified (Tarkir Dragonstorm) - $45
**Source:** Nitpicking Nerds

**Why it stands out:**
- $120 total value for $45 (2.5x ratio)
- Flurry spellslinger with flying/vigilance commander
- **Won consistently** when played against other Dragonstorm precons
- Average mana value 3.13 - not clunky at all
- Rite of Replication is "the hammer of the deck"

---

### Squirreled Away / Hazel (Bloomburrow) - $65
**Source:** Nitpicking Nerds

**Why it stands out:**
- Unique token aristocrats with **3 free sac outlets** (rare for precons!)
- 4 ways to pump for go-wide finishes
- Top 5 reprints worth $73 combined
- Best art cohesion - "feels like a storybook"
- Bello, Willowbloom, Plumb the Forbidden

---

### Phantom Premonition / Radar (Kaldheim) - $45
**Source:** Nitpicking Nerds

**Why it stands out:**
- Oldest precon on the recommended list but still solid
- $84 total value
- Foretell/blink/spirits hybrid
- Eerie Interlude worth $10 (20% of deck value)
- Radar triggers are **not once per turn** - scales quickly
- 6 ways to pump flyers for evasive damage

## Command Zone's Picks

### Best Overall Set of Precons 2024: **Bloomburrow**
- Clear vibe, knew exactly what they were doing
- Most consistent quality across all decks

### Best Quality (But Overpriced): **Modern Horizons 3**
- Tricky Terrain rated "S-tier"
- Eldrazi Unbound universally praised

### 2025 Sleeper Hit: **Sultai Arisen (Tarkir Dragonstorm)**
- Plays "gang busters" out of the box
- Best value retention of the year
- Obnix the Fallen, Life from the Loam reprints

### Other Notable Mentions:
- **Disa deck (MH3)** - "Absolute favorite deck of the year" for one Command Zone host
- **Jump Scare / Zamone (Duskmourn)** - Favorite commander design of 2024
- **Hasheton Scarab's Fist** - Best backup commander of 2025 by deck count

## Key Takeaways

1. **Modern precons are stronger than ever** - all top picks are from 2022-2025
2. **Universes Beyond precons** (Doctor Who, Warhammer 40k) punch above their weight
3. **Bloomburrow** is the "safest" set to buy from - all decks are solid
4. **Eldrazi Unbound** is the unanimous #1 for raw power
5. **Family Matters (Zenia)** offers the best price-to-quality ratio at $37
