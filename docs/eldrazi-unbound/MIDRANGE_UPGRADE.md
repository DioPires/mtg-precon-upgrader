# Resonance Engine (~$80-120)

*Trigger Doubling — Strong Bracket 3 with 2 Game Changers, lower price*

## Cards to ADD (10 cards)

### Game Changers (2 cards)

#### 1. Ancient Tomb
- **Type**: Land
- **Price**: $50-80
- **Effect**: Tap to add 2 colorless mana, deals 2 damage to you
- **Why Include**: Same reasoning as full upgrade - best colorless land, dramatically accelerates your game plan
- **Investment**: Worth it as it goes in every colorless deck forever

#### 2. The One Ring
- **CMC**: 4
- **Price**: $40-50
- **Effect**: ETB: Protection from everything until your next turn. Tap: Add a burden counter, then draw cards equal to burden counters. Lose 1 life per burden counter at upkeep.
- **Why Include**: Card draw is the biggest weakness of colorless decks. Solves this permanently while providing a turn of protection.
- **Verdict**: Arguably more important than Mana Vault for long-game consistency

---

### Eldrazi Titans (2 cards - most impactful ones)

#### 3. Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger
- **CMC**: 10
- **Price**: $30-40
- **Effect**: When cast, exile 2 target permanents. Indestructible. Attacks exile top 20 cards of defending player's library.
- **Why Include**: Exile 2 permanents on cast is the best cast trigger - hits ANY permanent type and doesn't target (gets around hexproof/shroud)
- **Bonus**: Mill-20 attack is a secondary win condition

#### 4. Kozilek, Butcher of Truth
- **CMC**: 10
- **Price**: $20-30
- **Effect**: When cast, draw 4 cards. Annihilator 4. When it dies, shuffle graveyard into library.
- **Why Include**: Draw 4 on cast refills your hand after dumping mana into big spells. Annihilator 4 closes games.
- **Value**: Better overall value than Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre for the price

---

### Utility & Value (6 cards)

#### 5. Eye of Ugin
- **Type**: Land
- **Price**: $8-12
- **Effect**: Colorless Eldrazi spells cost 2 less. Tap + 7 mana: Search for a colorless creature, put it in hand.
- **Why Include**: Cost reduction + tutor in a land slot. Essential for any Eldrazi deck.
- **Note**: The tutor ability is great late game when you have extra mana

#### 6. Sanctum of Ugin
- **Type**: Land
- **Price**: $2-3
- **Effect**: Tap for colorless. When you cast a 7+ CMC colorless spell, you may sacrifice this to search for a colorless creature.
- **Why Include**: Free tutor when you cast big spells. Sacrifice to find your next threat.
- **Synergy**: Works with the deck's natural game plan - you're already casting 7+ CMC spells constantly

#### 7. Conduit of Ruin
- **CMC**: 6
- **Price**: $1-2
- **Stats**: 5/5
- **Effect**: ETB: Search for a colorless creature with CMC 7+ and put it on top of your library. First creature spell each turn costs 2 less.
- **Why Include**: Tutors AND ramps. The 5/5 body is relevant too.
- **Cascade Synergy**: Perfect hit at 6 CMC - sets up your next cascade to hit the tutored creature

#### 8. Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter
- **CMC**: 3
- **Price**: $3-5
- **Stats**: 1/2 Flying
- **Effect**: Colorless spells you cast have flash. Gets +1/+1 counters when you cast spells based on mana spent.
- **Why Include**: Flash for all colorless spells changes how opponents have to play against you. They can never tap out safely.
- **Bonus**: Grows to massive size naturally

#### 9. Lithoform Engine
- **CMC**: 4
- **Price**: $5-7
- **Effect**: Multiple abilities including copying triggered/activated abilities (2 mana), copying instant/sorcery (3 mana), copying permanent spells (4 mana)
- **Why Include**: Copy any triggered or activated ability for 2 mana
- **Targets**: Copy cascade triggers (cascade twice more). Copy Annihilator. Copy Kozilek's draw 4. Copy Forsaken Monument's +2/+2 bonus.
- **Verdict**: Extremely versatile

#### 10. Strionic Resonator
- **CMC**: 2
- **Price**: $2-3
- **Effect**: Tap + 2 mana: Copy target triggered ability you control
- **Why Include**: Cheaper than Lithoform but only does triggered abilities. Still copies cascade, Annihilator, cast triggers.
- **Example**: 2 mana to copy Ulamog's "exile 2 permanents" is absurd value

## Key Combos & Synergies

### Combo 1: Double/Triple Cascade Value
**Cards**: Zhulodok + Lithoform Engine OR Strionic Resonator
- Cast a 7+ CMC spell with Zhulodok out → cascade, cascade
- Pay 2 + tap Strionic Resonator → copy one cascade trigger → cascade again (3 total)
- Or pay 2 with Lithoform Engine → same result
- With both artifacts? Copy BOTH cascades → 4 cascade triggers from one spell
- **Result**: Turn one spell into 5 spells

### Combo 2: Titan Trigger Doubling
**Cards**: Ulamog/Kozilek + Strionic Resonator + Lithoform Engine + Abstruse Archaic
- Cast Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger → exile 2 permanents trigger
- Copy with Strionic Resonator (2 mana) → exile 2 more
- Copy with Lithoform Engine (2 mana) → exile 2 more
- Copy with Abstruse Archaic (2 mana) → exile 2 more
- **Result**: Exile 8 permanents for 10 mana + 6 mana in copy costs

### Combo 3: Flash Engine
**Cards**: Liberator + Any big spell + The One Ring
- End of opponent's turn, flash in The One Ring
- Get protection until your turn
- Untap with full protection, draw cards, cast threats
- **Result**: Untap safely with card advantage

### Combo 4: Tutor Chain
**Cards**: Conduit of Ruin + Sanctum of Ugin + Eye of Ugin
- Turn 6: Cast Conduit of Ruin, tutor Ulamog to top of library
- Turn 7: Draw Ulamog, cast Ulamog (reduced by Eye of Ugin)
- Sacrifice Sanctum of Ugin on Ulamog cast → tutor Kozilek
- Turn 8: Cast Kozilek, draw 4, find more threats
- **Result**: Consistent threat chain every turn

### Combo 5: Annihilator Stacking
**Cards**: It That Betrays (already in deck) + Kozilek, Butcher of Truth + Strionic Resonator
- Attack with It That Betrays (Annihilator 2)
- Copy Annihilator trigger → opponent sacs 4 permanents
- You gain control of all sacrificed permanents (It That Betrays ability)
- **Result**: Opponent loses 4 permanents, you gain 4 permanents

### Combo 6: The One Ring Protection Timing
**Cards**: The One Ring + board wipes from opponents
- Cast The One Ring before your turn (with Liberator for flash, or just normally)
- "Protection from everything" prevents all damage, targeting, blocking
- Board wipes that say "destroy" or "deal damage" don't affect you
- Next turn: protection gone, but you've drawn cards and survived
- **Result**: Survive any non-exile board wipe

### Combo 7: Forsaken Monument + Copying
**Cards**: Forsaken Monument (already in deck) + Lithoform Engine
- Forsaken Monument gives all colorless creatures +2/+2 (static, not triggered)
- Forsaken Monument ALSO adds 1 mana when tapping colorless sources for mana (triggered)
- Copy the mana trigger with Lithoform Engine → extra mana
- Ancient Tomb now taps for 3 instead of 2
- **Result**: Your creatures are huge and you have more mana
