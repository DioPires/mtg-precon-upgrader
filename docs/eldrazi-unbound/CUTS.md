# Cards to CUT (10 cards)

These cards are cut in BOTH upgrade versions.

---

## X-Cost Spells (5 cards)

These spells don't work with Zhulodok's cascade ability. When you cascade into an X spell, X=0, making them useless.

### 1. Endless One
- **CMC**: X
- **Problem**: When you cascade into this, X=0, so it enters as a 0/0 and immediately dies
- **Impact**: Complete anti-synergy with Zhulodok's double cascade

### 2. Hangarback Walker
- **CMC**: XX
- **Problem**: Cascades as a 0/0 that dies immediately. The thopters-on-death won't trigger since it never had counters
- **Impact**: Wasted cascade trigger, no value

### 3. Stonecoil Serpent
- **CMC**: X
- **Problem**: X=0 on cascade means a 0/0 creature with protection from multicolored that dies on ETB
- **Impact**: Waste of a cascade trigger

### 4. Omarthis, Ghostfire Initiate
- **CMC**: X
- **Problem**: While it has manifest dread, cascading into it gives you a vanilla 2/2 manifest at best
- **Note**: The deck only has ~4 cards that care about +1/+1 counters - not enough support

### 5. Everflowing Chalice
- **CMC**: XX
- **Problem**: Cascades into a 0-mana rock with 0 charge counters
- **Impact**: Essentially a dead card that does nothing

## Underperforming Cards (5 cards)

These cards don't pull their weight in the deck's game plan.

### 6. Suspicious Bookcase
- **CMC**: 2
- **Stats**: 0/4
- **Problem**: Taps + 3 mana to make ONE creature unblockable
- **Better Option**: Rogue's Passage does this better and is already in the deck
- **Verdict**: Terrible rate for colorless

### 7. Transmogrifying Wand
- **CMC**: 3
- **Problem**: 3 mana + tap to exile a creature BUT it gives opponent a 2/4 ox
- **Limitations**: Only 3 uses
- **Better Options**: Duplicant and Meteor Golem are better and already in deck
- **Verdict**: Slow and helps opponents

### 8. Geode Golem
- **CMC**: 5
- **Stats**: 5/3 Trample
- **Problem**: MIGHT save you mana on commander if it deals combat damage
- **Limitations**: In a deck with 10-cost commanders, you need it to connect AND have your commander in hand
- **Verdict**: Too conditional

### 9. Scaretiller
- **CMC**: 4
- **Stats**: 1/4
- **Problem**: Only triggers when tapped/attacking or when it dies
- **Limitations**: In a deck wanting to cast big creatures, a 1/4 that slowly gets you lands is too slow
- **Verdict**: Better ramp options exist

### 10. Fireshrieker
- **CMC**: 3
- **Equip Cost**: 2
- **Problem**: Supports an equipment voltron strategy the deck doesn't commit to
- **Note**: You'd rather be casting Eldrazi than equipping creatures for double strike
- **Verdict**: Lightning Greaves is enough equipment
