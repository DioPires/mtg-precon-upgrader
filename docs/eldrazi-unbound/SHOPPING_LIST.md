## Budget Priority Order

If buying over time, prioritize in this order:

### Tier 1: Most Impact
1. **Ancient Tomb** - Goes in every colorless deck, massive acceleration
2. **The One Ring** - Solves colorless card draw problem permanently

### Tier 2: Win Conditions
3. **Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger** - Best Eldrazi cast trigger
4. **Kozilek, Butcher of Truth** - Draw 4 + Annihilator 4

### Tier 3: Consistency
5. **Eye of Ugin** - Cost reduction + late-game tutor
6. **Conduit of Ruin** - Tutor + cost reduction in one card

### Tier 4: Polish
7. **Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter** - Flash for everything
8. **Sanctum of Ugin** - Free tutor on big spells

### Full Upgrade Only:
9. **Mana Vault** - Explosive starts
10. **Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre** - Third titan

### Mid-Range Only:
9. **Lithoform Engine** - Versatile copying
10. **Strionic Resonator** - Cheap trigger copying

## Where to Buy

- **TCGPlayer** (tcgplayer.com) - Best for singles, competitive pricing
- **Card Kingdom** (cardkingdom.com) - Reliable, good condition grades
- **Star City Games** (starcitygames.com) - Premium seller
- **eBay** - Can find deals, verify seller ratings
- **Local Game Store** - Support local, immediate pickup

## Price Check Notes

Prices fluctuate based on:
- Recent tournament results
- New set releases
- Reprint announcements
- Commander format popularity

Always check current prices before purchasing. Prices in this guide are estimates as of late 2025.

## Post-Upgrade Checklist

After making the swaps:

- [ ] Deck has exactly 100 cards
- [ ] Verify at https://brackcheck.com/
- [ ] Game Changer count correct (Full: 3, Mid-Range: 2)
- [ ] No mass land destruction
- [ ] No early two-card infinite combos
- [ ] Test the deck in 2-3 games
- [ ] Adjust land count if needed (start with same count, adjust based on playtesting)
