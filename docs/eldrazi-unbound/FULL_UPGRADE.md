*All Titans Unite — Maximum Bracket 3 power with all 3 Game Changers*

## Cards to ADD (10 cards)

### Game Changers (3 cards - Maximum for Bracket 3)

#### 1. Ancient Tomb
- **Type**: Land
- **Price**: $50-80
- **Effect**: Tap to add 2 colorless mana, deals 2 damage to you
- **Why Include**: The best colorless land ever printed. In a deck trying to cast 7-10 mana creatures ASAP, this is invaluable.
- **Example Start**: Turn 1: Wastes, Sol Ring. Turn 2: Ancient Tomb = 5 mana available
- **Verdict**: Dramatically accelerates your game plan

#### 2. Mana Vault
- **CMC**: 1
- **Price**: $40-60
- **Effect**: Tap to add 3 colorless mana. Doesn't untap normally. Deals 1 damage during upkeep if tapped. Pay 4 to untap.
- **Why Include**: Pay 1 mana, get 3 mana next turn. Enables explosive starts.
- **Example Start**: Turn 1 Vault → Turn 2 play a 5-drop while having 2 mana from lands
- **Note**: Yes, it deals damage, but you're trying to end games with Eldrazi, not grind

#### 3. The One Ring
- **CMC**: 4
- **Price**: $40-50
- **Effect**: ETB: Protection from everything until your next turn. Tap: Add a burden counter, then draw cards equal to burden counters. Lose 1 life per burden counter at upkeep.
- **Why Include**: The most powerful colorless card draw engine. In colorless, card draw is HARD to find.
- **Verdict**: Solves the card advantage problem permanently

### Eldrazi Titans (3 cards)

#### 4. Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger
- **CMC**: 10
- **Price**: $30-40
- **Effect**: When cast, exile 2 target permanents. Indestructible. Attacks exile top 20 cards of defending player's library.
- **Why Include**: The cast trigger alone is worth 10 mana - 2-for-1 removal that can't be countered by countering Ulamog
- **Bonus**: Mill win condition as backup

#### 5. Kozilek, Butcher of Truth
- **CMC**: 10
- **Price**: $20-30
- **Effect**: When cast, draw 4 cards. Annihilator 4. When it dies, shuffle graveyard into library.
- **Why Include**: Draw 4 refills your hand after casting big spells. Annihilator 4 devastates boards.
- **Bonus**: Graveyard shuffle prevents mill losses

#### 6. Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre
- **CMC**: 11
- **Price**: $25-35
- **Effect**: When cast, destroy target permanent. Indestructible. Annihilator 4. When put into graveyard from anywhere, shuffle graveyard into library.
- **Why Include**: Cast trigger destroys any permanent type. 11 CMC is good with cascade - rarely cascade into higher CMC.
- **Bonus**: Graveyard shuffle clause

### Utility & Value (4 cards)

#### 7. Eye of Ugin
- **Type**: Land
- **Price**: $8-12
- **Effect**: Colorless Eldrazi spells cost 2 less. Tap + 7 mana: Search for a colorless creature, put it in hand.
- **Why Include**: Both ramp AND tutor in one land slot
- **Impact**: Kozilek costs 8. Ulamog costs 8 or 9.
- **Verdict**: Absurdly good

#### 8. Sanctum of Ugin
- **Type**: Land
- **Price**: $2-3
- **Effect**: Tap for colorless. When you cast a 7+ CMC colorless spell, you may sacrifice this to search for a colorless creature.
- **Why Include**: Chain your threats - cast Artisan of Kozilek, sac Sanctum, find Ulamog for next turn
- **Verdict**: Lands that tutor are always premium

#### 9. Conduit of Ruin
- **CMC**: 6
- **Price**: $1-2
- **Stats**: 5/5
- **Effect**: ETB: Search for a colorless creature with CMC 7+ and put it on top of your library. First creature spell each turn costs 2 less.
- **Why Include**: Tutors your best threat AND reduces its cost
- **Bonus**: Cascade can hit this at exactly the right value

#### 10. Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter
- **CMC**: 3
- **Price**: $3-5
- **Stats**: 1/2 Flying
- **Effect**: Colorless spells you cast have flash. Gets +1/+1 counters when you cast spells based on mana spent.
- **Why Include**: Flash Eldrazi are terrifying - hold up mana, see what opponents do, then drop a titan at end of turn
- **Bonus**: Grows huge naturally in this deck

## Key Combos & Synergies

### Combo 1: Explosive Mana Start
**Cards**: Ancient Tomb + Mana Vault + Sol Ring + any 2-cost rock
- Turn 1: Land, Sol Ring
- Turn 2: Ancient Tomb, Mana Vault (tapped)
- Turn 3: Untap Vault (or don't), you have access to 7+ mana
- **Result**: Cast Zhulodok on Turn 3, start cascading Turn 4

### Combo 2: The Cascade Chain
**Cards**: Zhulodok, Void Gorger + Any 7+ CMC spell + Conduit of Ruin
- Cast Zhulodok (commander with cascade, cascade)
- Each 7+ CMC spell you cast gets double cascade
- Conduit of Ruin puts your next bomb on top of library
- Sanctum of Ugin finds another threat when you cast a 7+ CMC spell
- **Result**: One big spell turns into 3-4 spells per turn

### Combo 3: Copy All The Triggers
**Cards**: Strionic Resonator OR Lithoform Engine + Any Titan
- Cast Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger → exile 2 permanents
- Copy the cast trigger with Strionic Resonator → exile 2 more (4 total)
- Or copy Kozilek, Butcher of Truth's trigger → draw 8 cards instead of 4
- **Result**: Double value on already devastating abilities

### Combo 4: Cascade Into Protection
**Cards**: The One Ring + Cascade
- High enough CMC (4) to be a good cascade hit
- If you cascade into it, you get protection from everything until your next turn
- Your board can't be wiped, you can't be damaged
- **Result**: Safe board development with free protection

### Combo 5: Annihilator Lock
**Cards**: Multiple Titans + Abstruse Archaic (already in deck)
- Abstruse Archaic can copy triggered abilities of colorless creatures for 2 mana
- Annihilator 4 triggers on attack → copy it → opponent sacs 8 permanents
- Combine with Strionic Resonator → 12 permanents sacrificed from one attack
- **Result**: One attack = game over for defending player

### Combo 6: Eye of Ugin Cost Reduction Chain
**Cards**: Eye of Ugin + Multiple Eldrazi
- Eye of Ugin reduces colorless Eldrazi costs by 2
- Cast Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger for 8 mana instead of 10
- Cast It That Betrays for 10 instead of 12
- Stack with Conduit of Ruin (-2 more) = Ulamog for 6 mana
- **Result**: Cast multiple titans per turn in late game

### Combo 7: Flash Eldrazi Ambush
**Cards**: Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter + Any Eldrazi
- Liberator gives all colorless spells flash
- Hold up mana, opponent attacks, flash in It That Betrays
- Block, steal everything that dies
- Or flash in Flayer of Loyalties (already in deck) to steal attacker
- **Result**: Opponents can't safely attack you
