## Cards to ADD (15 cards)

### Damage Amplifiers (3 cards)

#### 1. Wound Reflection
- **CMC**: 6
- **Type**: Enchantment
- **Price**: $8-12
- **Effect**: At the beginning of each end step, each opponent loses life equal to the life they lost this turn.
- **Why Include**: Even without the combo, this doubles all damage your group slug effects deal
- **Synergy**: Every ping, every drain, every damage source becomes twice as effective
- **Verdict**: Core card for any damage-focused strategy

#### 2. Solphim, Mayhem Dominus
- **CMC**: 4
- **Type**: Creature - Phyrexian Horror
- **Price**: $5-8
- **Stats**: 5/4
- **Effect**: If a source you control would deal noncombat damage to an opponent or a permanent an opponent controls, it deals double that damage instead. Indestructible ability.
- **Why Include**: Doubles ALL your noncombat damage - every ping, drain, and punishment effect
- **Synergy**: Stacks with other doublers. 1 damage → 2 (Solphim) → 4 (Wound Reflection)
- **Verdict**: Incredible value for 4 mana

#### 3. Fiery Emancipation
- **CMC**: 6
- **Type**: Enchantment
- **Price**: $10-15
- **Effect**: If a source you control would deal damage to a permanent or player, it deals triple that damage instead.
- **Why Include**: TRIPLES all damage, including combat. The most powerful damage multiplier.
- **Math**: 1 damage → 3 (Fiery) → 6 (Solphim) → 12 (Wound Reflection)
- **Verdict**: Win condition on its own

### Continuous Punishment (3 cards)

#### 4. Painful Quandary
- **CMC**: 5
- **Type**: Enchantment
- **Price**: $3-5
- **Effect**: Whenever an opponent casts a spell, that player loses 5 life unless they discard a card.
- **Why Include**: Taxes opponents for playing the game. Either they lose cards or life.
- **Synergy**: The 5 life loss triggers The Lord of Pain. With Wound Reflection = 10 life per spell.
- **Verdict**: Forces opponents into lose-lose situations

#### 5. Sulfuric Vortex
- **CMC**: 3
- **Type**: Enchantment
- **Price**: $2-4
- **Effect**: At the beginning of each player's upkeep, Sulfuric Vortex deals 2 damage to that player. Players can't gain life.
- **Why Include**: Constant damage tick AND shuts down lifegain strategies
- **Synergy**: 2 damage per turn × 3 opponents = 6 triggers for Lord of Pain
- **Anti-Synergy Warning**: Also damages YOU - plan accordingly
- **Verdict**: Aggressive clock that punishes slow strategies

#### 6. Zo-Zu the Punisher
- **CMC**: 3
- **Type**: Creature - Goblin Warrior
- **Price**: $3-5
- **Stats**: 2/2
- **Effect**: Whenever a land enters the battlefield, Zo-Zu the Punisher deals 2 damage to that land's controller.
- **Why Include**: Punishes ramp strategies, deals damage passively
- **Math**: Each opponent plays 1 land per turn = 6 damage per round (2 × 3 opponents)
- **Synergy**: With damage doublers, each land drop becomes devastating
- **Verdict**: Incidental damage adds up fast

### Pinger Synergy (2 cards)

#### 7. Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might
- **CMC**: 4
- **Type**: Creature - God
- **Price**: $8-12
- **Stats**: 4/4 Trample
- **Effect**: If a source you control would deal noncombat damage to an opponent, it deals 4 damage to that player instead.
- **Why Include**: Turns every 1-damage ping into 4 damage. Broken with Sulfuric Vortex.
- **Example**: Sulfuric Vortex deals 2 → becomes 4 (Ojer) → 8 (Wound Reflection)
- **Verdict**: Transforms weak pings into real threats

#### 8. Ob Nixilis, Captive Kingpin
- **CMC**: 4
- **Type**: Creature - Demon
- **Price**: $2-4
- **Stats**: 4/3 Flying, Trample
- **Effect**: Whenever one or more opponents each lose exactly 1 life, put a +1/+1 counter on Ob Nixilis and exile the top card of your library. You may play that card this turn.
- **Why Include**: Card advantage engine from all your small pings. Grows huge.
- **Synergy**: Sulfuric Vortex, pingers, small drains all trigger this
- **Note**: "Exactly 1 life" means you need sources that deal 1 at a time
- **Verdict**: Turns incidental damage into card advantage

## Key Combos & Synergies

### Combo 1: Pinger Amplification
**Cards**: Ojer Axonil + Any 1-damage source
- Sulfuric Vortex deals 2 damage to each player
- Ojer Axonil sets minimum to 4 damage
- Result: 4 damage per opponent per upkeep
- With Wound Reflection: 8 damage per opponent per upkeep
- **Damage Per Round**: 24 damage dealt to opponents (8 × 3)

### Combo 2: The Multiplication Stack
**Cards**: Solphim + Fiery Emancipation + Wound Reflection
- Any damage source deals X damage
- Fiery Emancipation: X × 3
- Solphim (noncombat only): X × 3 × 2 = X × 6
- Wound Reflection: X × 6 × 2 = X × 12
- **Example**: 1 base damage = 12 life lost
- **Example**: Sulfuric Vortex's 2 damage = 24 life lost per opponent

### Combo 3: Continuous Pressure Engine
**Cards**: Sulfuric Vortex + Descent into Avernus + The Lord of Pain
- Each upkeep: Vortex deals 2 to each player
- Your upkeep: Descent deals X to each player, gives treasures
- The Lord of Pain: Deals damage equal to first spell's MV to chosen player each turn
- **Result**: Every turn you're dealing 6+ damage across all opponents

### Combo 4: Spell Tax Punishment
**Cards**: Painful Quandary + The Lord of Pain + Wound Reflection
- Opponent casts a spell
- Painful Quandary: Lose 5 life or discard
- If they lose life: Lord triggers (deals CMC damage, you draw)
- End step: Wound Reflection doubles it (10 life total)
- **Result**: Casting a spell costs 10+ life or a card

### Combo 5: Ob Nixilis Value Engine
**Cards**: Ob Nixilis, Captive Kingpin + Any 1-damage pinger
- Deal 1 damage to each opponent
- Ob Nixilis triggers for EACH opponent losing exactly 1 life
- Exile 3 cards, put 3 +1/+1 counters, play any of those cards
- **Result**: Turn pings into massive card advantage

### Combo 6: Zo-Zu Land Punisher
**Cards**: Zo-Zu the Punisher + Damage multipliers
- Opponent plays a land
- Zo-Zu deals 2 damage
- Solphim: 4 damage
- Fiery Emancipation: 12 damage
- Wound Reflection: 24 damage
- **Result**: Playing a land deals 24 life loss

## Why No Infinite Combo?

**Punisher's Paradise trades the combo for:**
1. More damage multipliers (4 vs 2)
2. More continuous damage sources
3. Anti-lifegain (Sulfuric Vortex)
4. Land punishment (Zo-Zu)
5. $40-80 in savings

**The trade-off:**
- Pain Amplified can instantly kill one player with the combo
- Punisher's Paradise kills all opponents simultaneously through accumulated damage

**Which is better?** Depends on your meta:
- If opponents have lots of removal: Punisher's Paradise (more redundancy)
- If games go long: Pain Amplified (combo closes games)
- If you're on a budget: Punisher's Paradise (obviously)
