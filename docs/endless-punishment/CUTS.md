# Cards to CUT (15 cards)

These cards are cut in BOTH upgrade versions.

---

## Underperformers (5 cards)

These cards don't synergize well with The Lord of Pain's group slug strategy.

### 1. Combustible Gearhulk
- **CMC**: 6
- **Stats**: 6/6 First Strike
- **Problem**: The mill mode rarely benefits you in group slug, and the damage is random
- **Impact**: Opponents will always choose to mill if they have a stacked top-of-library
- **Better Use**: Those 6 mana should go toward damage multipliers

### 2. Massacre Wurm
- **CMC**: 6
- **Stats**: 6/5
- **Problem**: -2/-2 to opponents' creatures is situational - doesn't affect big threats
- **Limitation**: The 2 damage per death trigger is nice but requires board state
- **Verdict**: Too slow and conditional for reliable damage output

### 3. Rakdos, Lord of Riots
- **CMC**: 4 (BBRR)
- **Stats**: 6/6 Flying, Trample
- **Problem**: Requires you to have dealt damage to an opponent THIS TURN before casting
- **Limitation**: Telegraphed threat - opponents know what's coming
- **Note**: The cost reduction doesn't help if you're being controlled
- **Verdict**: Anti-synergy with being the archenemy (you will be)

### 4. Fear of Burning Alive
- **CMC**: 4
- **Type**: Enchantment Creature
- **Problem**: Slow, requires creatures to attack into it
- **Impact**: Doesn't proactively deal damage like group slug wants
- **Verdict**: Passive card in an active strategy

### 5. Sadistic Shell Game
- **CMC**: 3
- **Problem**: Political card that relies on opponents making choices
- **Limitation**: Random outcomes, opponents can game the system
- **Impact**: You want consistent damage, not random chance
- **Verdict**: Replace with reliable damage sources

## Slow Lands (6 cards)

The mana base is critical in Rakdos - you need to be deploying threats consistently.

### 6. Exotic Orchard
- **Type**: Land
- **Problem**: In a 2-color deck, this is unreliable
- **Limitation**: If opponents are mono-color or don't share your colors, this taps for nothing useful
- **Verdict**: Replace with dual lands that actually work

### 7. Terramorphic Expanse
- **Type**: Land
- **Problem**: Enters tapped, fetches a basic that ALSO enters tapped
- **Impact**: You're two turns behind on mana development
- **Verdict**: Too slow for an aggressive strategy

### 8. Bloodfell Caves
- **Type**: Land
- **Effect**: ETB gain 1 life
- **Problem**: Enters tapped, the 1 life is meaningless
- **Impact**: Group slug wants to be dealing damage, not gaining 1 life
- **Verdict**: Strictly worse than untapped duals

### 9. Evolving Wilds
- **Type**: Land
- **Problem**: Same issue as Terramorphic Expanse - double-tapped
- **Verdict**: Too slow

### 10. Geothermal Bog
- **Type**: Land
- **Problem**: Enters tapped, surveil 1 is minor upside
- **Impact**: You'd rather have mana available on curve
- **Verdict**: Budget filler, upgrade to untapped lands

### 11. Temple of the False God
- **Type**: Land
- **Problem**: Dead card until you have 5+ lands
- **Limitation**: In a deck that wants to curve out, a dead land is devastating
- **Verdict**: Unreliable, often stuck in hand early game

---

## Weak Rocks/Removal (4 cards)

### 12. Fellwar Stone
- **CMC**: 2
- **Problem**: In 2-color, this might not produce the colors you need
- **Better Option**: Wayfarer's Bauble guarantees a basic land untapped
- **Verdict**: Unreliable mana source

### 13. Thought Vessel
- **CMC**: 2
- **Problem**: No maximum hand size is rarely relevant in this deck
- **Impact**: You're spending cards as fast as you draw them
- **Better Option**: Ramp that actually accelerates you
- **Verdict**: The "no max hand size" is a trap

### 14. Rakdos Signet
- **CMC**: 2
- **Problem**: Requires mana to activate, doesn't ramp on turn 2 effectively
- **Better Option**: Arcane Signet already in deck, add better ramp
- **Verdict**: Redundant rock when better options exist

### 15. Feed the Swarm
- **CMC**: 2
- **Type**: Sorcery
- **Problem**: Sorcery speed, only destroys (doesn't exile), loses life
- **Better Option**: Withering Torment - instant speed, exiles, same life loss
- **Verdict**: Strictly worse than the replacement
