## Budget Priority Order

If buying over time, prioritize in this order:

### Tier 1: Core Strategy (Both Versions)
1. **Wound Reflection** ($8-12) - Essential damage doubler
2. **Crypt Ghast** ($4-6) - Mana + drain in one card
3. **Descent into Avernus** ($3-5) - Accelerates games

### Tier 2: Pain Amplified Priorities
4. **Grievous Wound** ($3-5) - Combo piece, great standalone
5. **Sorin Markov** ($4-6) - Game-ending threat
6. **Archon of Cruelty** ($5-8) - Big value creature

### Tier 2: Punisher's Paradise Priorities
4. **Fiery Emancipation** ($10-15) - Triples all damage
5. **Solphim, Mayhem Dominus** ($5-8) - Doubles noncombat
6. **Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might** ($8-12) - Minimum 4 damage

### Tier 3: Mana Base (Pain Amplified)
7. **Blood Crypt** ($15-20) - Best Rakdos dual
8. **Bloodstained Mire** ($30-40) - Premium fixing
9. **Luxury Suite** ($8-12) - Usually untapped

### Tier 3: Continuous Damage (Punisher's Paradise)
7. **Painful Quandary** ($3-5) - Taxes all spells
8. **Sulfuric Vortex** ($2-4) - Constant clock
9. **Zo-Zu the Punisher** ($3-5) - Land punishment

### Tier 4: Polish (Both Versions)
10. **Withering Torment** ($1-2) - Better removal
11. **Wayfarer's Bauble** ($1-2) - Reliable ramp
12. Remaining lands

## Price Check Notes

Prices fluctuate based on:
- Recent tournament results
- New set releases
- Reprint announcements
- Commander format popularity

Always check current prices before purchasing. Prices in this guide are estimates as of late 2025.

**Note on Bloodstained Mire**: This is the most expensive card in Pain Amplified. If budget is tight, you can substitute with:
- Prismatic Vista ($20-25) - Fetches any basic
- Fabled Passage ($3-5) - Budget fetch
- Another basic land - Free!
