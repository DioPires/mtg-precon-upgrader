## Key Synergies

### All Combos from Mid-Range PLUS:

### Infinite Combo: Grave Titan Loop
**Cards**: Ashnod's Altar + Nim Deathmantle + Grave Titan

1. Cast Grave Titan, create 2 zombies on ETB
2. Sacrifice Grave Titan to Ashnod's Altar (get 2 mana)
3. Deathmantle trigger - pay 4 to return Grave Titan
4. Grave Titan ETB - create 2 more zombies
5. Sacrifice 2 zombies to Altar (get 4 mana)
6. Repeat

**Result**: Infinite 2/2 zombie tokens, infinite colorless mana

### Game Changer Synergies

**Bolas's Citadel + Low Curve**
- Your deck has many low-CMC artifacts
- Chain multiple plays off the top of your library
- Life is a resource, especially with Wurmcoil Engine lifegain

**Necropotence + Imotekh**
- Draw a new hand every turn
- Discard excess cards to graveyard-filling (but Necropotence exiles discards)
- Use the raw card advantage to find combo pieces

**Demonic Tutor**
- 2 mana to find any combo piece
- Get Nim Deathmantle when you have Altar
- Get Altar when you have Deathmantle
- Find Bolas's Citadel for card advantage
