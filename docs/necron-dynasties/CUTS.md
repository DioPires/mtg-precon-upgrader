# Cards to CUT (12-15 cards)

These cards are underperforming or have poor synergy with Imotekh's artifact recursion strategy.

---

## Mid-Range Cuts (12 cards)

These cards are cut in the Mid-Range upgrade version.

### Underperforming Creatures (7 cards)

#### 1. Shadow of Mortality
- **CMC**: 15 (or 2 with low life)
- **Stats**: 7/7
- **Problem**: Requires you to be at low life to cast efficiently
- **Limitation**: Doesn't synergize with artifacts or recursion at all
- **Verdict**: A beat stick that doesn't fit the game plan

#### 2. Frogmite
- **CMC**: 4 (reduced by artifacts)
- **Stats**: 2/2
- **Problem**: Vanilla creature with no abilities
- **Limitation**: No death trigger, no ETB, just stats
- **Verdict**: Affinity is nice but the payoff is terrible

#### 3. Sojourner's Companion
- **CMC**: 7 (reduced by artifacts)
- **Stats**: 4/4
- **Problem**: Better in dedicated affinity decks
- **Limitation**: Vanilla body with artifact land cycling
- **Verdict**: Not worth the slot when there are better options

#### 4. Cathodion
- **CMC**: 3
- **Stats**: 3/3
- **Problem**: Mana on death has awkward timing
- **Limitation**: You get 3 colorless when it dies, but often can't use it effectively
- **Verdict**: The mana rarely matters when you need it

#### 5. Meteor Golem
- **CMC**: 7
- **Stats**: 3/3
- **Problem**: 7 mana for single removal is way too slow
- **Impact**: Better removal exists at much lower costs
- **Verdict**: Overcosted for what it does

#### 6. Psychomancer
- **CMC**: 4
- **Type**: Necron Wizard
- **Problem**: Low impact for the slot
- **Limitation**: Forcing discard is fine but not what this deck wants
- **Verdict**: Doesn't advance the game plan

#### 7. Triarch Praetorian
- **CMC**: 4
- **Stats**: 3/3
- **Problem**: Underwhelming stats and abilities
- **Limitation**: Menace and deathtouch are nice but not impactful enough
- **Verdict**: Better creatures available

---

### Artifacts (2 cards)

#### 8. Gate to the Afterlife
- **CMC**: 3
- **Problem**: Slow, inefficient tutor that exiles itself
- **Limitation**: Requires creatures to die AND you to be at 6+ creatures in graveyard
- **Impact**: Then it exiles itself to fetch God-Pharaoh's Gift (not in deck!)
- **Verdict**: This card is literally useless without its payoff card

#### 9. Morbid Curiosity (Sorcery)
- **CMC**: 3
- **Problem**: One-shot draw with awkward timing
- **Limitation**: Sorcery speed means you can't respond to removal
- **Impact**: Sacrificing an artifact just to draw cards is inefficient
- **Verdict**: Better card advantage options exist

---

### Lands (3 cards)

#### 10. Reliquary Tower
- **Type**: Land
- **Problem**: Actually HURTS the deck
- **Limitation**: You WANT to discard to graveyard for Imotekh triggers
- **Impact**: No maximum hand size prevents you from filling your graveyard
- **Verdict**: Anti-synergy with your commander

#### 11. Desert of the Glorified
- **Type**: Land
- **Problem**: Enters tapped
- **Limitation**: Cycling is nice but doesn't help your strategy
- **Impact**: You'd rather have a basic Swamp that enters untapped
- **Verdict**: Too slow

#### 12. Barren Moor
- **Type**: Land
- **Problem**: Enters tapped
- **Limitation**: Same issue as Desert of the Glorified
- **Impact**: Cycling doesn't justify the tempo loss
- **Verdict**: Too slow

---

## Full Upgrade Additional Cuts (3 more cards)

These cards are ALSO cut in the Strongest Bracket 3 version.

#### 13. Metalwork Colossus
- **CMC**: 11 (reduced by noncreature artifacts)
- **Stats**: 10/10
- **Problem**: Clunky, hard to reliably cast early
- **Limitation**: Recursion from graveyard is nice but costs too much
- **Verdict**: Replace with Grave Titan for better combo potential

#### 14. Wayfarer's Bauble
- **CMC**: 1 (+ 2 to activate)
- **Problem**: Fine card, but better options exist at this budget
- **Limitation**: Slot is better used for Demonic Tutor
- **Verdict**: Ramp is good but tutoring is better

#### 15. Myr Enforcer
- **CMC**: 7 (reduced by artifacts)
- **Stats**: 4/4
- **Problem**: Vanilla body with no abilities
- **Limitation**: Same issue as Frogmite but bigger
- **Verdict**: Replace with Necropotence for actual card advantage
