## Where to Buy

### Online Retailers
- [TCGPlayer](https://www.tcgplayer.com/) - Best for price comparison
- [Card Kingdom](https://www.cardkingdom.com/) - Reliable, fast shipping
- [CoolStuffInc](https://www.coolstuffinc.com/) - Good prices
- [StarCityGames](https://starcitygames.com/) - Premium grading

### Tips
1. **Check multiple sellers** on TCGPlayer for best prices
2. **Buy from same seller** when possible to save on shipping
3. **Near Mint vs Lightly Played** - LP saves money for casual play
4. **Set doesn't matter** - Any printing works for Commander
5. **Watch for sales** - Holiday sales often have 10-20% off
